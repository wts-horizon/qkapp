Privacy Policy — QK App

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Applies to: QK App (internal bookkeeping integration for QuickBooks Online)

1. Who we are

QK App ("the App") is a private, internal software tool operated by WTS Horizon Supply Chain LLC, 8 The Green, Ste A, Dover, DE 19901, USA ("we", "us"). The App is used solely by our own staff to automate bookkeeping tasks in our own QuickBooks Online company. It is not distributed, sold, or made available to any third party.

2. What data the App accesses

With the account holder's explicit OAuth 2.0 consent, the App accesses only the following data from our own QuickBooks Online company, under the scope com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting:

  • Chart of accounts, journal entries, invoices, bills, expenses, and payments;
  • Standard financial reports (profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance);
  • Basic company profile information (company name, fiscal year settings).

The App does not access payroll data, customer payment instruments, or any QuickBooks Online company other than our own.

3. How data is used and stored

Data retrieved through the App is used exclusively to prepare and post accounting entries and to generate internal financial reports. Data is processed and stored only on company-controlled computers and company-controlled storage. We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer QuickBooks data to any third party. No data is used for advertising or profiling.

4. Data retention and deletion

OAuth tokens and locally cached data are retained only while the App remains authorized. Deleting the local token file, or disconnecting the App in QuickBooks Online (Settings → Connected apps), immediately ends all access. Authorized users may request deletion of locally stored records at any time.

5. Security

Access is limited to authorized finance personnel. Credentials and tokens are stored with file-system access restrictions. All communication with Intuit services uses encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Access tokens are short-lived and automatically rotated.

6. Your rights

Because the App operates only on our own company data, individual end-user personal data is not collected. If you believe the App has processed data relating to you and wish to inquire about it, contact us at the address above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves. The effective date above will be revised accordingly.
